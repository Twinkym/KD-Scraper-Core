# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

KD-Scraper-Core is currently under active development and does not yet provide stable production releases.

Security updates, fixes, and governance policies are applied to the latest development branch and future stable releases.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, misconfiguration, or potentially dangerous behavior related to the project, please report it responsibly.

Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities, sensitive findings, or exploit details before a proper review and mitigation process has been completed.

At this stage of the project, vulnerability reports should be handled privately through the repository maintainers.

---

## Responsible Disclosure

KD-Scraper-Core follows a responsible disclosure philosophy.

Researchers, contributors, and users are expected to:

- Avoid public disclosure of unpatched vulnerabilities.
- Avoid exposing sensitive information or third-party data.
- Provide enough technical detail to reproduce and validate findings responsibly.
- Respect legal and ethical boundaries during security research activities.

The project does not support offensive or unauthorized activities.

---

## Ethical Usage Policy

KD-Scraper-Core is designed for ethical automation, defensive OSINT, monitoring, research, and authorized data collection workflows.

The project must not be used for:

- Unauthorized access attempts.
- Credential harvesting.
- Malicious automation.
- Denial-of-service activities.
- Privacy violations.
- Bypassing authentication or security controls.
- Illegal scraping activities.

Users and contributors are responsible for complying with applicable laws, platform policies, and ethical standards.

---

## Security Principles

KD-Scraper-Core follows security-oriented engineering principles aligned with KD-Tech Universe standards:

- Secure-by-design architecture.
- Principle of least privilege.
- Modular isolation and maintainability.
- Ethical and transparent automation practices.
- Responsible data handling.
- Defensive engineering mindset.
- Continuous security awareness and improvement.
